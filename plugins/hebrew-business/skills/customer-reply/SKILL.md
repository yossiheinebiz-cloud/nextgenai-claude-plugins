# Customer reply in Hebrew

## Before writing
- Identify: question, complaint, price request, review (positive or negative), or lead.
- Find the facts you need (price, hours, policy). If the user did not give them, ask or leave [placeholder]. Never invent a policy, refund or discount.

## Write
- Open with the customer's name if known.
- Answer the actual question in the first sentence.
- Complaint: acknowledge the specific problem, say what you will do and by when, move details to a private channel. No blame, no excuses.
- Public review reply: short, thank, address one concrete point, no personal data.
- Lead: answer, then one question that moves to the next step (call, visit, quote).
- WhatsApp: 1 to 4 short lines. Email: up to 3 short paragraphs.

## Give the user
- One reply ready to send, plus one shorter alternative.
- A note if the message needs a human decision (refund, legal threat, health or safety).

Rules: plain spoken Hebrew, plural "we" for a business unless told otherwise, no em or en dash, no emojis unless the business uses them. Never send anything yourself; the user sends.
